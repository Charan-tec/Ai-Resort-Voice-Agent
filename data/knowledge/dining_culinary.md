# Resort Dining & Culinary Experiences

## Azure Ocean Grill
- Fine-dining Pacific Rim cuisine with oceanfront terrace seating.
- Dinner: 5:30 PM to 10:00 PM daily. Reservations recommended.
- Dress Code: Resort elegant.

## Palm Terrace Buffet & Breakfast
- Daily Gourmet Breakfast Buffet: 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM ($35 per adult, complimentary with breakfast package).
- Features made-to-order omelets, tropical smoothie bar, fresh local pastries, and Kona coffee.

## Sunset Lounge & Cocktail Bar
- Open 4:00 PM to midnight. Live acoustic music every evening from 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM.
- Handcrafted tropical cocktails, artisanal poke bowls, and tapas.
- Happy Hour: 4:30 PM to 6:00 PM with 30% off signature cocktails.

## In-Room Dining
- 24-hour room service available via speed-dial on guest phone.
