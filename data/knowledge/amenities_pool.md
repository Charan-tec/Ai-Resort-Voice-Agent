# Resort Amenities, Pools & Wellness

## Swimming Pools
- Infinity Lagoon Pool: Family-friendly, heated to 82°F. Open daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
- Serenity Adult Pool: Exclusively for guests 18+, quiet sanctuary with private cabanas. Open 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
- Private Poolside Cabanas: Include dedicated butler service, chilled towels, seasonal fruit platter, and sparkling wine. Rental fee: $250/day.

## Serenity Spa
- Hours: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily.
- Signature Treatments:
  - Hawaiian Lomi Lomi Massage (80 min): $240
  - Deep Tissue Restoration (60 min): $190
  - Couples Oceanfront Bliss Massage (90 min): $450
- Steam rooms, Finnish saunas, and plunge pools are complimentary for all resort guests.

## Fitness Center
- 24-hour access with room keycard. State-of-the-art Peloton bikes, free weights, and Technogym machines.
- Daily complimentary sunrise yoga sessions on the beach at 7:00 AM.
