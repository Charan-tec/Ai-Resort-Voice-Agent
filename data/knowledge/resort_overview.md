# Azure Paradise Resort - Overview & Policies

## Check-In & Check-Out
- Check-in time begins at 3:00 PM local time.
- Check-out time is strictly 11:00 AM local time.
- Late check-out may be requested up to 2:00 PM based on availability for a $50 late fee.
- Early check-in before 1:00 PM is subject to room availability upon arrival.

## Location & Transportation
- Address: 100 Paradise Cove Boulevard, Maui, Hawaii.
- Airport Shuttle: Complimentary luxury shuttle runs hourly between Kahului Airport (OGG) and the resort. Private transfers available for $80.
- Valet parking is available 24/7 for $35 per night. Self-parking is complimentary for registered guests.

## Cancellation & Refund Policy
- Flexible rate reservations may be cancelled with full refund up to 48 hours prior to the 3:00 PM check-in date.
- Cancellations made within 48 hours of check-in are subject to a one-night room and tax penalty.
- No-shows will be billed for the entire duration of the booked reservation.

## Pet Policy
- Pets are not permitted at Azure Paradise Resort.
- Certified service animals as defined by the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) are welcome with prior notification.
- Emotional support animals and comfort pets are not permitted on the resort property.

## Room Service & Dining
- 24-hour in-room dining is available for all registered guests via speed-dial on room phones.
- Azure Ocean Grill offers fine Pacific Rim cuisine from 5:30 PM to 10:00 PM daily.
- Palm Terrace offers gourmet breakfast buffet daily from 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM.
- Sunset Lounge & Cocktail Bar is open 4:00 PM to midnight with live acoustic music.

## Accommodations & Room Features
- Oceanfront Luxury Villa (villa-ocean-101): Max occupancy 4 guests, base rate $650 per night. Features private infinity pool, ocean view, king bed, dedicated butler service, and complimentary breakfast.
- Deluxe Sunset Suite (suite-deluxe-201): Max occupancy 2 guests, base rate $380 per night. Features panoramic sunset balcony, king bed, whirlpool spa tub, and espresso bar.
- Garden Tropical Bungalow (bungalow-garden-301): Max occupancy 3 guests, base rate $290 per night. Features private zen garden, open-air rain shower, queen bed, and hammock terrace.
- Presidential Ocean Suite (suite-presidential-401): Max occupancy 4 guests, base rate $850 per night. Features panoramic ocean terrace, master king bedroom, whirlpool spa tub, dedicated butler service, and complimentary champagne.
- Family Pool Villa (villa-family-501): Max occupancy 6 guests, base rate $520 per night. Features private swimming pool, sun deck & garden, two king beds & bunk suite, full kitchenette, and family entertainment center.
- Premium Garden Suite (suite-premium-302): Max occupancy 3 guests, base rate $450 per night. Features tropical garden view, king bed & daybed, marble soaking tub, espresso bar, and complimentary breakfast.

## Resort Policies & Activities
- Azure Paradise Resort is a 100% smoke-free and vape-free property.
- Resort quiet hours are observed from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM daily.
- Complimentary resort activities include daily 7:00 AM sunrise beach yoga, guided reef snorkeling tours, Hawaiian lei making workshops, and sunset catamaran sails.
